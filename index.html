Why With Insight

Large organisations are hard to see whole. Strategy, people, process, information and technology each change at a different pace, and each has a different owner. We build one clear view of how they fit, so leaders can decide what to change and in what order.

Who we work with
Executives and program leaders in large, complex organisations.
When we are engaged
Before a major investment, during a transformation, or when a program needs an independent review.
What we leave behind
A clear design, a plan to deliver it, and a team that can maintain both.

What we do

We work across four disciplines.

Most engagements use more than one. We combine them, so that the strategy, the design and the architecture agree.

01

Strategy development

Set a direction that people can act on.

We work with executives to define where the organisation is going and why. We test the options against evidence, cost and risk. The result is a strategy with clear priorities and an order of delivery.

Typical outputs

  • Strategic direction and priorities
  • Target state on a page
  • Roadmap
  • Investment case
  • Measures of success
02

Enterprise design

Design how the whole organisation works.

A strategy needs an organisation that can deliver it. We design the services, capabilities, structures and governance to do that. We design them together, because a change to one affects the others.

Typical outputs

  • Operating model
  • Capability model
  • Service and process design
  • Organisation and governance design
  • Transition plan
03

Architecture

Plan business and technology change as one design.

We describe the organisation as it is and as it needs to be. The description covers capabilities, information, applications and technology. We then set out the steps between the two, so every program builds to one agreed design.

Typical outputs

  • Enterprise and business architecture
  • Current and target state
  • Transition roadmap
  • Architecture governance
  • Design assurance
04

Consulting

Independent advice for important decisions.

Some problems need a senior, independent view for a short period. We review programs, facilitate executive decisions and assess designs that other teams produce. We also coach internal teams, so the capability stays in the organisation.

Typical outputs

  • Independent reviews
  • Executive workshops
  • Program shaping
  • Second opinions on major designs
  • Coaching for internal teams

How we work

Each engagement has four stages.

The stages are the same for a short review and a long program. The depth of each stage changes with the size of the work.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We start with your people, your documents and your data. We ask the difficult questions early, while the answers can still change the plan.

  2. 02

    Make sense

    We put the whole picture on one page. People then debate the same facts, and the points of disagreement become clear.

  3. 03

    Design

    We develop options with your team and test each one against cost, risk and capacity. We recommend one option and give the reasons.

  4. 04

    Mobilise

    We turn the design into a plan with owners, a sequence and funding. We stay involved as delivery starts, and we adjust the design as teams learn.

01 / 04Service delivery · Architecture

One blueprint for a large service transformation

Situation
A large organisation had funding for a transformation that would run for several years. Many projects were under way, and each one had its own view of the future.
Our work
We built one target state across business, information and technology. We mapped each project to it and set out the order of delivery.
Outcome
Executives used one blueprint to sequence investment and to resolve design disputes between programs.

02 / 04New organisation · Enterprise design

The design of a new organisation

Situation
A new organisation had a mandate, a start date and a small founding team. It needed to operate from its first day.
Our work
We defined its functions and capabilities, and designed the operating model. We identified the minimum set of services and systems it required at launch.
Outcome
The organisation started with a clear operating model and a staged plan to build the rest.

03 / 04Investment planning · Strategy

From a strategy to a funded roadmap

Situation
A department had a strategy that its leaders supported. The work had no agreed sequence, cost or owner.
Our work
We tested the priorities with executives and grouped the initiatives into stages. We then built the roadmap and the case for investment.
Outcome
Leaders agreed what to do first, what to defer and what to stop.

04 / 04Program review · Consulting

An independent review of a critical program

Situation
A technology program was behind schedule. Teams disputed the main design decisions, and executives were losing confidence.
Our work
We reviewed the architecture, the governance and the open decisions. We reported the findings in plain terms, with a recommended course for each.
Outcome
The executive made the outstanding decisions, assigned each risk to an owner and reset the delivery plan.

What to expect

Six commitments in every engagement.

01

The whole picture

We look across strategy, people, process, information and technology. Most problems cross those boundaries.

02

Plain English

We write for the people who must act on the work. If a page needs a glossary, we rewrite the page.

03

Evidence first

We form a view from documents, data and interviews. We show the evidence behind each recommendation.

04

Work that is used

We produce a small number of clear documents that teams use to decide. We do not write documents that nobody will use.

05

Alongside your team

We work with your people from the first week. They own the result, and they can maintain it after we leave.

06

Discretion
