How we work

Five links connect a strategy to its solutions.

We link strategy, capabilities, benefits, requirements and solutions. A reader can then trace each decision back to its evidence.

  1. 01

    Strategy

    Goals and measurable objectives set the direction.

  2. 02

    Capabilities

    A capability model shows what the organisation must be able to do. Each capability has a maturity rating.

  3. 03

    Benefits

    A benefits map links each change to the benefit it must deliver.

  4. 04

    Requirements

    We review the requirements against the architecture.

  5. 05

    Solutions

    We map each application to the capability it supports. A roadmap sets the order of delivery.

Selected work

Examples of our work, without the names.

Much of our work is sensitive. These examples name no client, program or person.

01 / 06A national agency · Current

Reference architecture for a new national capability

Need
One agreed design for a new national capability that the Commonwealth, states and territories share.
Work
We provide the lead architect for the program's reference business architecture. It covers strategy, value streams, a capability model with maturity ratings and the target operating model. It also covers the business view of data and an implementation roadmap. We produced the architecture decision records, a pain point register, the benefits management plan, a benefits map and the concept of operations. We drafted goals and measurable objectives for the agency's strategic plan. We reviewed the agency's draft data strategy and the program's requirements against the architecture. We now provide the business architecture inputs to the second pass business case.
Status
The work is in progress.

02 / 06A large federal department · Current

Evidence for an ERP replacement decision

Need
A sound basis for choosing how to replace the corporate ERP system.
Work
We provide the business analyst and architect for finance and procurement on the program team. We mapped the department's value chain and value streams. We helped build the framework for evaluating the options. We host current-state workshops with business areas and document their processes against a standard government process library. We also work on program scope, the operating model and governance, benefits, change impact and steering committee material.
Status
The work is in progress.

03 / 06A national regulator · Current

One view of services for a digital transformation

Need
One shared view of the regulator's services to guide a multi-year digital transformation.
Work
We provide a business architect for the transformation. We are the custodian of the master service catalogue. We designed the service blueprint and a matching view of the data behind each service. We started an inventory of current systems from existing documents. We drafted the governance cycle for future-state decisions, a data architecture approach and the team's work program.
Status
The work is in progress.

04 / 06A large federal department · Three years

Value streams and a capability model for a major department

Need
One authoritative view of how the department delivers value and which capabilities it needs.
Work
We built the enterprise value stream and a catalogue of operational value streams across every portfolio. We developed the baseline enterprise capability model. We contributed business architecture content to the ICT strategy and roadmap. We wrote a multi-year capability uplift strategy and investment roadmap for enterprise accounting.
Result
Every governance body endorsed the capability model as the department's authoritative business architecture view. The value stream catalogue became the department's framework for mapping capabilities and finding work to consolidate.

05 / 06Two national agencies

ICT strategies for two national agencies

Need
A business-led ICT strategy and a roadmap to implement it.
Work
We led a multi-disciplinary team that co-designed a user-centred ICT strategy and roadmap with the first agency. The strategy came with research insights, personas, journey maps and architecture views. We then delivered a concise ICT strategy and roadmap for the second agency.
Result
Both agencies received a strategy and an implementation roadmap. The second strategy took five weeks.

06 / 06A non-government school system

Operating model redesign for a school system

Need
An operating model that supports the core services the system offers.
Work
We led an evidence-based redesign of the operating model. We defined what stakeholders needed and the business capabilities required to deliver the core services.
Result
Leadership endorsed the transformation work plan, which was built on those capabilities.
